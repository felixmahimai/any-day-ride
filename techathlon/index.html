What happens next

From one event to a regular habit

The event

Corporate teams ride and run together, and hear how Sundays on Cycle works.

The club

Each participating company is encouraged to start a cycling group and register it on the Fit India portal.

The Sunday

Groups join Sundays on Cycle, and a company challenge around FIPL keeps them going.

What we request

Three things from Fit India

Logo as supporting partnerUsed as the Fit India Movement Logo Guidelines allow: unaltered, with clear space and minimum sizes.
Guidance on new clubsThe simplest way for a company starting from zero to set up and register a club on the portal.
Recognition for clubsA certificate or a mention on Fit India channels for clubs that join through this pathway.

What Fit India gets

More clubs, more Sundays

  • More corporate cycling clubs. New groups registered on the portal after each event.
  • Riders who keep riding. People move from one event to regular Sunday rides.
  • A company challenge around FIPL. A simple challenge keeps new groups active.
  • A pathway to repeat. The same steps work in each city Techathlon visits.
  • Clear numbers. Companies and riders onboarded, shared with participants' consent.

How it works
