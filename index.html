Streaks, and Bronze, Silver and Gold tiers by ride days. Certificates. Carbon credit points on the Fit India app. Leaderboards and FIPL prizes.

A Cycling-Friendly Workplace and Campus rating from Fit India, shown on a certificate and a plaque.
